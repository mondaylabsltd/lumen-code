Easier on the eyes while receiving, and photos from your library.

- The code being read is covered by a plain plate with the progress on it, so you are not looking at a flickering picture. The eye button shows the code again.
- The outline of the code stays steady while receiving.
- Choose a picture or a video from Photos to send, not only a file.
- When the cells of a code are too small for the camera, the app says to move closer, or to lower the density on the sender.
- Android: the outline follows a phone held on its side, and the focus is set once on the code and held.

Compatible with Protocol 1.0 and earlier 1.0 apps.
