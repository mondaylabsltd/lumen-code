Lumen Code — send files without internet.

Move files and text between a screen and a camera. No shared network, pairing or account is needed. Once installed, the app works fully offline.

SEND WHAT YOU NEED
Choose a file or type text on one device. Point the receiving phone at its screen. View received text and images, then save or share them. Suitable for documents, photos and configuration files; individual files can be up to 256 MiB.

MADE FOR REGULAR USE
The phone app typically receives faster than the web version. Clear progress and live speed show how the transfer is going. Missed frames do not mean starting over: keep scanning to collect more data.

WORKS ACROSS DEVICES
Transfer between phones, computers and the free web version at lumencode.link. Useful when devices cannot share a network or USB connection.

ONE PURCHASE
No subscription, advertising or in-app purchases. Final pricing is shown by the store.

LOCAL PROCESSING
The app makes no network connections and collects no data. Files and camera images are processed on your device. The protocol and source code are available under the MIT License.

BEFORE YOU TRANSFER
The displayed code is not encrypted or authenticated. Encrypt sensitive files first and check their source. The code changes colour up to 60 times a second; do not look at it if you are sensitive to flashing light. The app asks for confirmation before the first playback.
